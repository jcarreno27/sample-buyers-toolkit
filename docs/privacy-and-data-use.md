# Privacy and data use

Everything you paste into an AI chat tool leaves your machine and goes to
that tool's provider. Usually that's fine. Sometimes it isn't. Two minutes
of thought before pasting is the whole discipline.

## The rule of thumb

Paste specs and structures freely; think twice about **names, prices, and
respondent data**.

## Generally fine to paste

- Project specs: audiences, quals, n, LOI, IR assumptions, quotas, timelines
- Questionnaires and screeners (yours)
- Your own fielding metrics
- Supplier replies **with a moment's review first** (below)

## Think before pasting

**Client identity.** "Meridian Foods" doesn't need to be in the chat for
any agent to work. Alias it ("a food brand"). Client confidentiality
agreements often cover the *existence* of projects, not just data.

**Supplier commercial terms.** Quotes are often sent under explicit or
assumed confidentiality. Aliasing ("Supplier A/B/C") keeps the comparison
fully functional — the Bid Comparator genuinely does not care who's who.
What your NDA says beats anything this page says.

**People's details.** Strip supplier reps' names, emails, and phone numbers
from pasted replies. They don't affect any comparison.

## Never paste

**Respondent-level data.** Individual survey responses, open-end verbatims
with identifying detail, panelist IDs, or anything traceable to a person.
The agents only ever need aggregates ("drop-off 14%", "9 removals"), and
respondent data carries GDPR/CCPA and panel-terms obligations that pasting
into a chat tool can violate. Aggregate numbers are always enough.

## Know your tools' settings

Consumer AI tools differ on whether chats may be used for model training
and how long they're retained — and workplace/enterprise tiers differ from
free ones. Check your organization's AI policy and the tool's data
settings once, before the first real project. This kit has no opinion on
which tool you use; it just assumes you've made this check.

## The anonymization workflow (10 seconds per bid)

1. Paste the supplier reply into a scratch document
2. Replace the supplier name with "Supplier A" (and so on), delete rep
   names/contact details
3. Delete anything marked confidential that isn't a term you're comparing
4. Then paste into the agent

The comparison output stays exactly as useful — you know who Supplier A is.
