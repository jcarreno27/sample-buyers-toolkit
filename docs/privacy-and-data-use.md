**Sample Buyers Toolkit Privacy Policy**

Effective date: October 5, 2026

**Who provides this plugin**

Sample Buyers Toolkit is a skills-only plugin published by Jeremy Carreno. It provides instructions and reference materials for reviewing market-research briefs, supplier bids, questionnaires, fielding updates, and respondent-incentive scenarios.
This policy describes the toolkit package's data practices. It does not replace the privacy policies or account settings of ChatGPT, Codex, GitHub, or another application you choose to use.

**Information processed and why**

When you use a skill, your AI application processes the text, attachments, and task context you supply or select so it can perform the requested review or draft. This may include study specifications, audience definitions, commercial terms, aggregate fielding metrics, and any personal information you include, such as names or business contact details. Identifiable personal information is not needed for the toolkit's intended workflows.

The plugin does not require a separate toolkit account. It contains no developer-operated server, MCP connection, external database, analytics SDK, tracking pixel, or mechanism that sends your conversation or project files to Jeremy Carreno. Jeremy does not receive your chat content or attachments merely because you use the plugin.

Use aliases for client and supplier names where possible. Provide aggregate figures and blank questionnaires instead of identifiable respondent records. Do not supply passwords, API keys, payment-card details, government identifiers, protected health information, or identifiable respondent-level data. Only provide material you are authorized to use in your chosen AI application.

**Who receives information**

Your AI application provider processes the information you submit under its own terms, privacy policy, and applicable account or workspace settings. For use in ChatGPT or Codex, that provider is OpenAI. The toolkit itself does not send information to sample suppliers, Borderless Access, advertisers, data brokers, or a developer-controlled service, and it implements no sale or sharing of user information for advertising.

Visiting the project's GitHub pages or posting a support issue is a separate interaction with GitHub. Information you put in a public issue, including your GitHub username and issue text or attachments, can be seen by Jeremy and other members of the public. Do not post private project materials or personal records there. If you separately authorize another connected service in your AI application, that service's data practices also apply to that interaction.

**Retention**

The plugin has no separate developer-controlled storage for conversations, attachments, or generated results. It therefore maintains no developer-side copies or retention period for those materials. Copies held by the AI application, your workspace, or your device follow that provider's policies and your applicable settings; this policy does not promise immediate deletion or a particular retention period for those copies.
Public GitHub support issues remain available for as long as the issue content is retained on GitHub. There is no scheduled automatic deletion by this plugin. Editing or removing public content may not remove copies already obtained by other people or retained by GitHub under its policies.

**Your controls**

You choose which materials to provide. You can redact or omit information, stop using or uninstall the plugin, and use the AI application's available conversation, file, privacy, and data-deletion controls. For provider-held data, direct access or deletion requests to the relevant provider. Uninstalling the plugin does not itself delete existing conversations, files, or support posts.
For support information you posted on GitHub, use the editing and deletion controls available to your account or request assistance through the project contact below. Jeremy cannot delete copies maintained independently by OpenAI, GitHub, other users, or your organization.

**Contact and changes**

For questions about this policy or the toolkit, contact Jeremy Carreno through the project's GitHub issue tracker. That channel is public: provide only the minimum non-sensitive detail needed to describe your question.
Policy updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Any future version that adds developer-operated connections, storage, analytics, or other data handling will need an updated disclosure reflecting that functionality.
