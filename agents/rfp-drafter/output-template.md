# RFP Drafter — output template

What an RFP Drafter response should look like: four ready-to-paste emails
plus a pre-send checklist.

```
=== DETAILED RFP ===
Subject: Sample RFP — [audience shorthand], [market], n=[n] — response
  by [date]

[Greeting]

[1–2 sentences: what the project is and when responses are needed.]

WHAT WE NEED
[the full spec block: audience, quals, exclusions, market/languages, n,
LOI, assumed IR — labeled as our assumption, with a request for the
supplier's own estimate — quotas, device, hosting, timeline incl.
soft launch]

YOUR RESPONSE MUST INCLUDE
1. CPI (currency) and any minimum or additional fees
2. The IR and LOI assumptions your price is based on
3. What happens to price and timeline if actual IR is lower than assumed
4. Feasibility statement for the full spec — and what it's based on
5. [Per-cell feasibility for any cell of concern]
6. Sample sources and expected blend
7. Quota management approach, including the hardest cells
8. Data quality measures, and who absorbs the cost of removals
9. Expected fielding pace (day-by-day if possible) and soft-launch
   readout timing
10. Your PM contact and escalation path during fielding

[House rules / compliance block if provided]

[Deadline restated + how to send questions. Sign-off.]

=== SHORT RFP ===
Subject: Quick quote request — [audience shorthand], n=[n], field
  [dates]
[Same substance, ~1/3 the length, for suppliers who know the drill.]

=== FOLLOW-UP: CHASER ===
Subject: RE: [original subject] — response due [date]
[Two sentences: polite nudge, deadline restated, offer to answer
questions.]

=== FOLLOW-UP: MISSING INFO ===
Subject: RE: [original subject] — a few gaps in your response
[Thanks + "to compare bids fairly we need every response to cover the
same items" + numbered list of skipped items + revised deadline.]

=== BEFORE YOU SEND ===
- [open decisions in the brief]
- [reminder: identical spec to every supplier]
- [ASSUMPTION: anything the drafter had to fill in]
```
