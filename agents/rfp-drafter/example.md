# RFP Drafter — example

The Allergy Parents study at stage 3: the brief is confirmed (n=400 in
writing, quotas set at ~133 per age band, English-only — and the client
resolved the brief's open diagnosis-wording decision in writing:
parent-reported HCP diagnosis qualifies) and the feasibility review flagged
IR quality and the 13–17 cell. All names fictional.

## What the user pasted in

The confirmed brief, plus: responses due Friday June 5, 5pm ET; three
suppliers, mixed relationship; questions by email; show the deadline as hard;
and the four supplier questions from the
[Feasibility Checker example](../feasibility-checker/example.md).

## What the RFP Drafter returned (detailed RFP and chaser shown; short version and missing-info follow-up omitted here for length — the agent produces all four)

```
=== DETAILED RFP ===
Subject: Sample RFP — US parents, food-allergy children, n=400 —
  response by Fri Jun 5, 5pm ET

Hi [name],

We're bidding out sample for a US consumer study fielding June 11–21
(hard close — client commitment on June 24). Details below; we'd
appreciate your response by Friday June 5, 5pm ET.

WHAT WE NEED
- Audience: US parents/legal guardians of at least one child under 18
  who has a food allergy diagnosed by a healthcare professional
  (parent-reported diagnosis; self-diagnosis does not qualify)
- Market/language: US, English
- Completes: 400
- Quotas (not interlocked): allergic child's age 0–5 / 6–12 / 13–17,
  ~133 per band
- LOI: 15 minutes (estimated, not yet tested)
- IR: our client assumes 20%. We have not validated this and would
  genuinely value your own estimate and what it's based on.
- Device: survey must be fully mobile-compatible
- Hosting: we host and program (Forsta); you provide sample only
- Timeline: launch June 11 with a ~10% soft launch and a same-day
  IR/LOI readout; full launch on our go; hard close June 21

YOUR RESPONSE MUST INCLUDE
1. CPI in USD, plus any minimum or additional fees
2. The IR and LOI assumptions your price is based on
3. What happens to price and timeline if actual IR is materially lower
   than your assumption — please be specific
4. A feasibility statement for the full spec, and what it's based on
5. Feasibility for the 13–17 age-band cell specifically, within the
   10-day window
6. Sample sources and expected blend (own panel / partners / exchange)
7. Your quota management approach, including the cells you expect to be
   hardest
8. Data quality measures (fraud, dupes, speeders, attention checks) and
   who absorbs the cost of removals
9. Expected fielding pace across the 10 days, and how quickly after
   soft launch we get the readout
10. Your PM contact and escalation path during fielding

Questions by email any time before the response deadline — we'll answer
all bidders identically.

Thanks,
[signature]

=== FOLLOW-UP: CHASER ===
Subject: RE: Sample RFP — US parents, food-allergy children, n=400 —
  response by Fri Jun 5, 5pm ET

Hi [name] — a quick nudge that responses are due Friday 5pm ET. Happy
to answer any questions before then; otherwise we look forward to your
bid.

=== BEFORE YOU SEND ===
- Incentive handling wasn't in your brief — the RFP assumes
  supplier-handled (ASSUMPTION). Confirm or state otherwise before
  sending.
- Send the identical spec to all three suppliers, including the ones
  you know well — comparability dies the moment specs diverge.
- Item 3 (IR-adjustment consequences) is the one suppliers most often
  answer vaguely. If any response hand-waves it, use the missing-info
  follow-up — that answer matters more than the headline CPI.
```

## Why this example matters

The RFP states the client's 20% IR as an *assumption* and asks each supplier
for their own estimate — three expert feasibility opinions, free. Every
supplier answers the same ten numbered items, which is what makes the
[Bid Comparator](../bid-comparator/) possible. And there's no budget number
anywhere in it.
