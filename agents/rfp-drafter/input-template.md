# RFP Drafter — input template

Paste your brief (Brief Builder output drops straight in), plus the logistics
below. If the Feasibility Checker gave you "questions to put to suppliers,"
include them — they get worked into the RFP.

```
RFP REQUEST

--- THE BRIEF ---
[paste your sample brief here]

--- RFP LOGISTICS ---
Response deadline for suppliers: [date/time + timezone]
How many suppliers will receive this: [number — no names needed]
How will supplier questions be handled: [email / group call / none]
Show fielding deadline as hard? [yes/no — default yes]
House rules to attach (payment terms, compliance, PII):
Anything specific you want suppliers to address:
[e.g., feasibility questions from your feasibility review]

--- TONE ---
Relationship: [suppliers who know us / new suppliers / mixed]
```
