# Questionnaire Risk Reviewer — input template

This mirrors the kit-wide
[questionnaire review template](../../templates/questionnaire-review-template.md).
The single most important thing to include: the **routing/termination
logic**, not just the questions.

```
QUESTIONNAIRE REVIEW REQUEST

--- CONTEXT ---
Audience and quals:
Market(s):
n and quotas:
Assumed IR: [% and basis]
Claimed/estimated LOI: [minutes — measured or estimated?]
Device expectation: [e.g., majority mobile]
Incentive level (if known):
Can the questionnaire still change? [yes / barely / no]

--- WHAT I MOST WANT CHECKED ---
[e.g., "is the screener leaking quals?", "is LOI realistic?",
"will the grids survive mobile?" — or "everything"]

--- SCREENER ---
[paste in full: question text, answer options, and who terminates/
routes where]

--- MAIN SURVEY ---
[paste in full or the sections to review; include grids (with row/
column counts), open-ends, and any media/stimuli descriptions]
```
