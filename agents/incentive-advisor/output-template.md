# Incentive Advisor — output template

What a recommendation should look like. Three absolutes: the anchor is
shown as arithmetic with the comp assumption labeled, the calibration
regime is stated up front, and **the regime gates the numbers** — for a
consumer audience without user-supplied calibrated norms, or an HCP
audience without fair-market-value/compliance guidance, the RECOMMENDED
RANGE and tier table are omitted entirely (the agent gives direction,
cautions, and the questions that get calibrated numbers instead — it never
invents a range to satisfy the format).

```
INCENTIVE RECOMMENDATION — [project]
Regime: [B2B (calibrated) / consumer (direction only) / HCP (compliance
  flag first)]

THE ANCHOR (arithmetic — challenge any line)
ASSUMPTION: approximate compensation $[X]/yr
÷ 2,080 hours ≈ $[Y]/hr × ([LOI] min ÷ 60) ≈ $[Z] base time value

RECOMMENDED RANGE: $[lo]–$[hi] per complete
| Tier | Amount | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | $ | flexible timeline, clean screener |
| Compressed | $ | tight timeline or niche vertical |
| Premium | $ | named companies, credentials, verification-heavy |

ADJUSTMENTS APPLIED
- Sourcing ([method]): ×[range] — [why]
- Function scarcity: [above/below average — why]
- Stacked qualifiers ([n]): [loading applied; 3+ also = sourcing flag]
- Floor: [applied at $X / not needed]

CAUTIONS
- [low-end engagement risk for active/EN sourcing]
- [hardest-cell rule: price to $[X] for the [cell] cell]
- Incentive ≠ CPI — your CPI adds recruitment, screening, PM, and
  margin on top of this. If a quoted CPI is close to this incentive,
  ask what the respondent actually receives.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE THIS
- [missing facts, priority order]

ASK YOUR SUPPLIER
1. What does the respondent actually receive, in what form (cash /
   points / donation), and how quickly after completing?
2. [who funds a mid-field bump / per-cell incentives / ...]
```
