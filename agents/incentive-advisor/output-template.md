# Incentive Advisor — output template

What a recommendation should look like. Three absolutes: the anchor is
shown as the arithmetic (B2B) or the length-and-sourcing basis (consumer)
that fits the regime, with any comp assumption labeled; the regime is
stated up front; and **the regime picks the numbers**. B2B and consumer
both get a range, labeled a starting point to adjust with the user's own
norms (patients price as consumers). HCP audiences get no dollar figure at
all: the agent hands back the fair-market-value anchor (specialty hourly
rate prorated by LOI) and the compliance question, and never invents an HCP
number to satisfy the format.

```
INCENTIVE RECOMMENDATION — [project]
Regime: [B2B (opportunity-cost) / consumer (effort-anchored) /
  HCP (FMV-disciplined)]

THE ANCHOR (show the one that fits the regime)
- B2B: approx. comp $[X]/yr ÷ 2,080 ≈ $[H]/hr × ([LOI] min ÷ 60) ≈ $[Y] time value
- Consumer: basis ~[LOI] min, [habitual gen-pop panel / directly recruited]
- HCP: FMV anchor = [specialty] hourly rate × ([LOI] min ÷ 60); the rate,
  the cap, and the reporting call come from your compliance team

RECOMMENDED RANGE: $[lo]-$[hi] per complete
(HCP: no figure; hand back the FMV anchor for compliance to price)
| Tier or case | Amount | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| B2B: Standard / Compressed / Premium, or consumer: gen-pop panel / directly recruited | $ | ... |

ADJUSTMENTS APPLIED
- Sourcing / access ([method]): [why] (expert-network and marketplace
  multipliers are B2B-only; for consumer, note panel-set vs. direct-recruit)
- Scarcity / qualifiers: [loading; rarity lands in CPI, not incentive]
- Floor: [applied at $X / not needed]

CAUTIONS
- [low-end engagement risk; underpaying selects for junk]
- [hardest-cell rule: price to $[X] for the [cell] cell]
- Incentive is not CPI: your CPI adds recruitment, screening, PM, and
  margin on top. If a quoted CPI is close to this incentive, ask what the
  respondent actually receives.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE THIS
- [missing facts, priority order]

ASK YOUR SUPPLIER
1. What does the respondent actually receive, in what form (cash /
   points / gift card / donation), and how quickly after completing?
2. [who funds a mid-field bump / per-cell incentives / ...]
```
