# Feasibility Checker — output template

What a Feasibility Checker response should look like. Risk is always
Low / Medium / High with a reason — never a percentage, never a verdict.

```
FEASIBILITY RISK ASSESSMENT — [project name]

OVERALL RISK: [Low / Medium / High]
[One paragraph: the main driver(s) of the rating, in plain English.]

RISK TABLE
| Dimension | Risk | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Audience rarity | L/M/H | ... |
| Market depth | L/M/H | ... |
| n vs. audience | L/M/H | ... |
| Quota structure | L/M/H | ... |
| LOI | L/M/H | ... |
| Timeline | L/M/H | ... |
| IR assumption quality | L/M/H | ... |

THE RISKIEST COMBINATION
[One paragraph on the interaction most likely to break the project —
not the worst single dimension, the worst *combination*.]

WHAT WOULD CHANGE THIS ASSESSMENT
- [the missing facts that matter most, in priority order]

MITIGATIONS (in order of impact)
1. [mitigation + what it costs you (time, cells, client conversation)]
2. ...

QUESTIONS TO PUT TO SUPPLIERS
1. [questions that would verify or kill the concerns above]
2. ...
```

Funnel arithmetic, when shown, appears as *"the arithmetic your own
assumptions imply"* — illustration, never prediction.
