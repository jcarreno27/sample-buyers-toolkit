# Supplier response paste template

How to feed supplier replies to the **Bid Comparator**. Paste each reply
between the markers, exactly as received (email text is fine — the agent does
the normalizing). Remove or alias anything you shouldn't share; consistent
aliases like "Supplier A" work fine.

Before pasting, consider stripping: supplier employees' names and contact
details, your client's name, and anything the supplier marked confidential.
See docs/privacy-and-data-use.md.

```
BID COMPARISON REQUEST

Project spec (paste your brief or the key numbers):
[audience, market, n, LOI, assumed IR, quotas, timeline]

What matters most to us on this project (pick/rank):
[price / speed / feasibility confidence / quality / all of it]

=== SUPPLIER A — [alias or name] ===
[paste their full reply here]
=== END SUPPLIER A ===

=== SUPPLIER B — [alias or name] ===
[paste their full reply here]
=== END SUPPLIER B ===

=== SUPPLIER C — [alias or name] ===
[paste their full reply here]
=== END SUPPLIER C ===

Anything you know that isn't in the replies (past experience with
these suppliers, verbal caveats from calls, etc.):
[optional — the agent will treat this as your input, not fact]
```
