# Questionnaire review template

What to give the **Questionnaire Risk Reviewer**. The agent reviews for
sample and fielding risk — IR, LOI, quotas, mobile, fraud-bait — not for
research methodology.

```
QUESTIONNAIRE REVIEW REQUEST

--- CONTEXT ---
Audience and quals:
Market(s):
n and quotas:
Assumed IR: [% and basis]
Claimed/estimated LOI: [minutes]
Device expectation: [e.g., majority mobile]
Incentive level (if known):

--- WHAT I MOST WANT CHECKED ---
[e.g., "is the screener leaking quals?", "is LOI realistic?",
"will Q12's grid survive mobile?" — or "everything"]

--- SCREENER ---
[paste the full screener, question by question, with answer
options and routing/termination logic]

--- MAIN SURVEY ---
[paste the full survey, or the sections you want reviewed;
include grids, open-ends, and any media/stimuli descriptions]
```

Tip: include the *routing logic* (who terminates where), not just the
questions. Half of screener risk lives in the routing.
