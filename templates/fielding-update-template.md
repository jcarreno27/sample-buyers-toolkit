# Fielding update template

What to paste into the **Fielding Risk Advisor** while a project is in field.
Numbers beat adjectives — "quota cell is slow" is less useful than "13–17 cell
has 12 of 130 after 4 days." Fill in what you have; mark the rest "unknown."

```
FIELDING UPDATE — [project name] — Day [X] of [Y planned]

--- TARGETS & BOUGHT ASSUMPTIONS ---
Total completes needed: [n]
Fielding window: [start date] to [deadline] — [hard/soft]
IR the project was awarded on: [%]
LOI the project was awarded on: [minutes]

--- ACTUALS SO FAR ---
Completes to date: [x of n]
Actual IR (if reported): [%]
Actual median LOI: [minutes]
Drop-off/abandon rate: [%]
Quality removals so far: [count or % — and reason if known]
Over-quota rate (if reported): [%]

--- QUOTA STATUS ---
[cell]: [x of target]
[cell]: [x of target]
[cell]: [x of target]

--- SUPPLIER SIGNALS ---
Last substantive supplier update: [when, and what they said]
Anything the supplier has asked to change (IR, price, timeline, quals):
Soft launch done? What did it show?

--- YOUR READ ---
What are you worried about, if anything?
What decisions are you facing right now?
```
