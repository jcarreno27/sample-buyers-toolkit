# Supplier RFP template

The structure of a quotable RFP. The **RFP Drafter** agent produces emails in
this shape; you can also fill it in by hand. A supplier who gets all of this
can quote accurately in one pass — which is the whole point.

```
SAMPLE RFP — [project name]

Response requested by: [date/time + timezone]

--- WHAT WE NEED ---
Audience: [one plain-English sentence]
Qualification criteria:
  - [criterion 1]
  - [criterion 2]
Exclusions: [if any]
Market(s): [country/ies, languages]
Completes: [n, split by market if applicable]
LOI: [X minutes — state whether measured or estimated]
Assumed IR: [X% — state the basis for this estimate]
Quotas: [list; flag any interlocks explicitly]
Device: [e.g., must be mobile-compatible]
Survey hosted by: [us / you / third party]

--- TIMELINE ---
Target launch: [date]
Fielding window: [X days / hard deadline and why]
Soft launch: [yes — X% first, pause for review / supplier's standard]

--- WHAT WE NEED FROM YOUR RESPONSE ---
1. CPI (state currency) and any minimum fees
2. The IR and LOI assumptions your price is based on — state the IR's
   base (general-population entrants vs. a pre-targeted frame)
3. What happens to price/timeline if actual IR is lower than assumed
4. Feasibility statement: can you deliver this n in this window?
   What's your confidence based on?
5. Per-cell feasibility for any quota cell we've flagged as a concern
6. Sample sources: own panel, partners, exchange? Expected blend?
7. Quota management approach, including the hardest cells
8. Data quality measures (fraud, dupes, speeders, attention checks)
   and who bears the cost of removals
9. Fielding plan: expected completes by week (or by day), and
   soft-launch readout timing
10. Your PM contact and escalation path during fielding

--- HOUSE RULES ---
[Payment terms, security/compliance requirements, PII rules, etc.]
```
